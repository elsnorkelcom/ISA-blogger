Tactical & Historical Summary

BAP Abtao (originally Tiburón) served the Marina de Guerra del Perú from 1954 to 1999. In 1988, she participated in the heroic rescue of survivors from BAP Pacocha after its collision. Preserved as a floating museum ship in the port of Callao since 2004.
